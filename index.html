How a work order moves

A job isn't complete when the contractor leaves. It's complete when the photos, paperwork and your requirements all check out.

  1. Receive

    We review the scope, deadline and your client-specific rules before anything goes out.

  2. Assign

    We match the job to a vetted local contractor by trade, location and availability.

  3. Track

    We follow up until the work is done and flag delays before they become missed deadlines.

  4. Check

    We review photos and documentation against your requirements and send back anything incomplete.

  5. Report

    You get a clean submission and invoice, ready for your records or your client.

About Bastion Fieldworks

Bastion Fieldworks LLC is an Ohio limited liability company that coordinates property maintenance and preservation work for property managers and property owners.

Our founder comes from U.S. property preservation operations, with hands-on experience in vendor management, work-order coordination, photo review and quality control. We built Bastion around what that work taught us: most problems in the field come from unclear scopes, slow follow-up and incomplete documentation, so that's what we manage most closely.
